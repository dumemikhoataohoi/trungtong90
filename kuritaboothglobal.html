ABOUT KURITA BOOTH GLOBAL

A new sales system, not a translated website

KURITA BOOTH GLOBAL is the overseas Paint Booth platform of 株式会社栗田工業 (Kurita Kogyo Co., Ltd.), a Japanese manufacturer with years of experience in automotive body-repair paint equipment. Phase 1 focuses entirely on the KURITA MAX SERIES, starting in Vietnam.

This is deliberately narrow: while Kurita Kogyo also builds drying ovens, mixing equipment, local exhaust systems and hazardous-material storage, the entry point for ASEAN is fixed at Paint Booth = KURITA MAX SERIES — so the first thing the market learns is a single, strong idea.

We are not translating a Japanese website. We are building a new ASEAN sales system for KURITA MAX.
